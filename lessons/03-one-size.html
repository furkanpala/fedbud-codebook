Lesson 3

Why one model does not fit all

Each client can spend a different amount of computation. One shared model must fit the client with the smallest budget, and the other clients then leave most of their budget unused.

The idea

A language model reads text as tokens, short pieces of words. Every token costs computation. A client's budget is the share of the full model's cost per token that the client can spend. A hospital with a server may afford most of the full cost. A clinic with a laptop may afford a small share.

Lesson 2 showed that federated averaging needs every client to hold the same model. So in plain federated learning, the shared model must be cheap enough for the client with the smallest budget. The other clients could run a larger, better model, but they do not get one.

Questions

Question 1

Three clients have budgets of 0.3, 0.6 and 0.9. What is the largest cost of one shared model that all three can run?

0.3, the smallest budget. A larger model would not run on the rural clinic's computer.

Question 2

With that shared model of cost 0.3, what fraction of its budget does the hospital with a budget of 0.9 leave unused? Give a number between 0 and 1.

About 0.67. The hospital uses 0.3 of its 0.9, so it leaves 0.6 of 0.9 unused, which is two thirds of its budget.

Question 3

What would you give each client instead of one shared model?

Each client could get its own sub-network, a part of one large model sized to its budget. The hospital would keep most of the large model and the clinic a small part. Lesson 4 shows how to cut such a model, and Lesson 8 shows how the clients can still train together when their models differ.

Summary

    
Clients differ in how much computation they can spend on each token. One shared model must fit the smallest budget, so the clients with larger budgets leave much of it unused. The answer of the FedBUD paper is to give each client its own sub-network, a part of one large model sized to the client's budget. An open question is how to choose that part for each client and how to train the different parts together without sharing data.
